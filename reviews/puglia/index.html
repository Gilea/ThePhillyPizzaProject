Puglia Pizza

The sauce is the best thing on the menu. If the cheese matched it, this would be a serious contender.

What We Ordered

Three 12" pies, cooked to order

Margherita

⭐ Best of the night
GA

The tomato sauce is the best thing on this pizza — possibly the best sauce we've had on this project. Fresh oregano stands out in exactly the right way. The crust is crispy on the bottom, which I appreciate. The cheese lets it down: underwhelming, needs more salt. The cooked basil actually works — it captures the flavor fully. This pizza with proper fresh mozzarella would be a showstopper. As it is, it's still my favorite of the night.

MW

That sauce is incredible. The oregano is a really nice touch — you actually taste it. The cheese needs to be better for this to reach its potential. But the base is there. This is our best pizza of the night, no question.

MA

The sauce is outstanding. Fresh basil cooked into it — you get the full flavor rather than raw herb on top. The cheese is the weak link: it doesn't add much and the pizza as a whole needs more salt. Replace the cheese with proper fresh mozzarella and this changes completely. Still our top pick of the evening.

Pepperoni

GA

Very good. Right amount of salt, good cheese coverage, excellent pepperoni. Slightly dry — it could use more sauce, like the margherita has. They have a hot honey pepperoni that I'd want to try next time. That might push this into a different category.

MW

This one works. The pepperoni is well seasoned and the cheese-to-sauce ratio is mostly right. I like this one a lot. The hot honey version sounds like something worth coming back for.

MA

Solid pepperoni. A little dry compared to the margherita, and it could use a bit more sauce underneath. Still a good slice — second best of the night.

White Pizza — Truffle Ricotta

GA

The mushrooms are delicious and the garlic tastes genuinely fresh — that part works. I can smell the truffle oil but the truffle flavor doesn't carry through to the taste. The ricotta makes the whole thing heavy. Double the mushrooms, use real truffles instead of oil, and it would be great. As it stands, it's the weakest of the three.

MW

I can't detect any truffle at all. The mushrooms are the best thing on here. The garlic is good. But the ricotta is too much — it overwhelms the other flavors. Not a fan of this one.

MA

The garlic is great, the mushrooms are tasty, and the ricotta gives it body — but you can tell the truffle is from oil, not the real thing. It smells like truffle on arrival; it just doesn't taste like it. Worth trying once, but I'd skip it on a return visit.

A note on the experience

Sit-down dining, but you order at the counter — which is fine. Paper plates are a minor annoyance for a sit-down spot. The bottle of water instead of a glass felt like a cut corner. At $16–20 per 12" pie, the price is genuinely good for Philadelphia. They also sell slices — including Roman style with a thicker crust — which is worth noting if you want to stop in without committing to a full pie.

The Verdict

Worth a visit

"Order the margherita. Come back for the hot honey pepperoni. Bring your own glass expectations."

Puglia has one ingredient that's genuinely exceptional: the tomato sauce. It's bright, well-seasoned, and the fresh oregano makes it taste intentional. Everything else is in service of that — sometimes well, sometimes not. The cheese is the obvious weak point; it neither adds flavor nor texture, and it costs the margherita what would otherwise be a top-tier pizza.

The pepperoni is reliable and well-executed. The truffle ricotta white pizza has good components — mushrooms, fresh garlic — but the truffle is an illusion of oil rather than the real thing, and the ricotta makes it heavier than it needs to be.

Good value for South Street. The kind of place you come back to on a Tuesday without making a reservation. The hot honey pepperoni is the unfinished business.
