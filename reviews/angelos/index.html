Angelos

Ordered online, picked up, drove 15 minutes home. The crust was still super crispy. That tells you most of what you need to know.

"Order online. Skip the line. Reheat in the oven."

The logistics: Online order, pickup only — no dining in. The app showed 15 more minutes of wait time when we arrived, but the pies were ready. After a 15-minute drive home, the crust was still crispy. Not greasy. Large pies at $28–30 each. One important note: the pizza is noticeably better reheated — next time, straight into the oven before eating.

Pizzas Tried

1. White with Fungi & Sausage

A white pizza loaded with mushrooms, sausage, and roasted garlic cloves. The mushrooms are genuinely good — cooked through, worth ordering. The garlic divided the panel: GA found it too much, MA thought the balance was right. The roasted garlic cloves on top are a nice touch. A rich, heavy slice that splits opinion.

GA"This one is a little too garlicky for me. The mushrooms are good, but the garlic overpowers everything else."—
MW"It's a lot of cheese for a slice of pizza. The mushrooms are good — they're cooked through and worthwhile."—
MA"I think there's the right amount of garlic and the right amount of cheese. The roasted garlic cloves on top are a nice touch."—
2. Trenton ⭐ Best of the night

A traditional cheese and tomato pizza, and the best of the three. The sauce is excellent — tomatoes on top of tomato sauce, balanced sweetness, right amount of salt. The crust holds up. MA called it near perfect. Sometimes the simplest pizza is the one that reveals what a place can really do.

GA"Really good crust. The sauce is tasty. Simple and well executed."—
MW"The tomato sauce is really good. Clean, balanced. This is a solid pizza."—
MA"I really like this version of a traditional cheese pizza. The tomatoes together with the tomato sauce are very good — the perfect balance of sweetness, right amount of salt. Really good crust. Near perfect cheese and tomato pizza, in my opinion."—
3. The Sixth Man

Half pepperoni cups, half sausage — a pizza for people who want both. The pepperoni is genuinely excellent but the quantity is extreme: GA found it ridiculous, MW removed a third of his. The remarkable thing is that despite the amount, the slice isn't overly spicy or greasy. The sausage half is sweet and well-seasoned. Basil leaves on top are a nice finishing touch.

GA"Really, really good pepperoni. But it's a ridiculous amount of pepperoni for one slice. Too much. That said — the amazing thing is it's not overly spicy at all, even with all those cups."—
MW"I took off about a third of the pepperonis from my slice. The sausage half has a lot of sausage too, but it's really good — sweet and tasty. Right amount of crispy crust."—
MA"Great balance for pepperoni and sausage lovers. I agree there's too much pepperoni, but the overall slice is not overly spicy. The sausage is sweet and tasty. I like the basil leaves on top — great pizza."—

The Verdict

Angelos is a serious pizza. The crust holds up after a 15-minute drive without losing its crispiness — which is not easy. The Trenton is the standout: clean tomato sauce, fresh tomatoes, right salt balance, near-perfect in its simplicity. The Sixth Man has outstanding pepperoni buried under an overwhelming quantity of it; it takes self-restraint to eat it properly, but the flavor is there. The white with fungi is divisive, but the mushrooms are worthwhile.

Both GA and MW noted they'd score higher had they eaten it immediately. MA's 8.6 with reheating in mind probably reflects the ceiling accurately. The unanimous advice: order online, pick it up, and put it straight in the oven when you get home. That's the full experience.

"Order online. Skip the hour-long line. Reheat in the oven. Order the Trenton."
